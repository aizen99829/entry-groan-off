# The One-Liner Groan-Off

An entry for the make.fun bounty "The One-Liner Groan-Off": one original
dad joke, one line, under 140 characters.

## The entry

> The electrician charged me for the repair, and I was shocked — but he said that was just the current rate.

106 characters. One line. Three electric puns stacked in a single sentence.

## Why this joke

A good dad joke earns a groan, not a laugh — the listener has to feel
slightly betrayed by a pun they saw coming half a second too late.
This one works because the setup sounds like an ordinary complaint
about a repair bill, so the first pun ("charged") slips past unnoticed.
By the time "shocked" lands, the theme is obvious, and "current rate"
is the third hit that makes the reader groan out loud. The joke is
written fresh for this entry; it is not a recycled classic.

## How it runs

`main.py` validates the joke (exactly one line, at most 140 characters),
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and prints it with its character count:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## Rules followed

- One line, one dad joke, 106 characters — inside the 140 limit.
- Original wordplay, written for this competition.
- No borrowed punchlines, no explanations, no extra lines.
